# Speculative-stock research brief

Use the full company name followed by the ticker everywhere a company is introduced or labeled. Do not use bare tickers as company names.

## Disposition

`researchable | watch only | highly speculative | avoid pending new evidence | insufficient evidence`

State the single most important risk and the `as of` timestamp.

## What is verified

List the 3-5 facts that matter most, each with a direct source and date.

## Discovery ranking

For ticker-optional or broad opportunity searches, rank no more than five companies. State the selected objective: `risk-adjusted quality | catalyst upside | maximum speculation`. Include sector/theme, why it ranks here, principal risk, and the primary-source event that could change its rank.

## Capital structure and financing risk

Show current shares, known dilution, registered financing capacity, authorized headroom, recent issuance, reverse-split history, and conflicting share-count sources. Cite every financing or share-count row inline; allow no orphan facts in an end-only source list.

## Business and solvency

Summarize operating proof, revenue/cash-flow trend, customer/backlog quality, cash runway, debt, going concern, related parties, and management alignment.

## Reddit/social audit

Summarize the narrative, claim-ledger result, coordination signals, disclosure quality, and the strongest verified bull and bear comments. Do not reproduce hype.

## Bull / base / bear

For each scenario, state milestones, share-count assumption, capital required, invalidators, and liquidity constraint. Avoid unsupported probabilities and price targets.

## Scorecard

Rate `strong`, `mixed`, `weak`, or `unknown`: evidence quality; capital-structure safety; solvency health; operations/management; catalyst quality; market integrity/tradability.

## Unknowns and next trigger

Name the missing evidence, the exact document/event that could change the disposition, and when it is expected. End with sources, linked directly and ordered by authority.
