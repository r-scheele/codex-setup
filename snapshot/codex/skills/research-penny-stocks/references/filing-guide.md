# Filing and capital-structure guide

## Read first

| Need | U.S. filing/source | What to extract |
|---|---|---|
| Annual business and risks | 10-K, 20-F, 40-F | business, risk factors, audited statements, controls, going concern |
| Quarterly update | 10-Q, 6-K | cash, burn, share count, debt, subsequent events |
| Material event | 8-K, 6-K | financing, contract, default, delisting, split, auditor, acquisition |
| New securities | S-1/F-1, S-3/F-3, EFFECT, 424B | maximum amount, selling holders, use of proceeds, dilution, warrants |
| Ownership/control | 3/4/5, 13D/G, DEF 14A | open-market buys vs grants, beneficial ownership, votes, compensation |
| OTC status/actions | OTC Markets, FINRA OTC Daily List | reporting tier, warnings, shell flag, split/symbol/corporate action |

For non-U.S. issuers, use the home regulator first: SEDAR+ and SEDI in Canada, Companies House/FCA/RNS in the UK, ASX releases in Australia, or the relevant exchange and securities regulator. Reconcile foreign filings with U.S. ADR/ADS filings.

## High-value filing sections

- cover page share count and public float date
- liquidity and capital resources
- risk factors and going-concern language
- debt, convertibles, derivatives, warrants, equity compensation, related parties, commitments, and subsequent events footnotes
- exhibits for financing agreements, customer contracts, employment terms, and amendments
- dilution table, selling-shareholder table, plan of distribution, use of proceeds, and incorporation-by-reference documents

## Dilution audit

Separate:

1. current outstanding common shares
2. known issuable shares from in-the-money or contractually convertible instruments
3. performance/vesting-contingent shares
4. registered resale shares owned by existing holders
5. primary offering capacity that can raise issuer cash
6. authorized but unissued headroom

Do not treat resale registration as fresh cash to the issuer unless the prospectus says the issuer receives proceeds, such as from warrant exercise. Do not add an ATM twice when it uses an existing shelf.

Inspect conversion price, floor, reset, full-ratchet/weighted anti-dilution, cashless exercise, alternative conversion, default premium, make-whole, commitment shares, placement-agent warrants, and ownership blockers. Model at more than one share price when conversion is variable.

## Insider activity

Distinguish open-market purchases from grants, restricted awards, option exercises, tax withholding, gifts, and conversions. Compare dollar size with compensation and ownership. A purchase supports alignment; it does not validate the business claim.

## Reverse splits

A reverse split changes share count and per-share price mechanically, not enterprise value. Check the stated purpose, compliance deadline, authorized shares after the split, historical post-split issuance, fractional-share treatment, and whether financing follows the action.

## Filing conflict rule

When sources disagree, use the newest filing that reports the relevant measurement date, then explain why a later press release or data vendor may differ. Preserve both `filed date` and `as of date`.
