# Source map

Use the newest authoritative source available and timestamp every retrieval.

## Primary sources

- SEC EDGAR company and full-text search: https://www.sec.gov/search-filings
- SEC microcap research and fraud guidance: https://www.investor.gov/additional-resources/spotlight/microcap-fraud
- FINRA low-priced-stock and short-interest guidance: https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/nothing-micro-about-microcap-fraud and https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/short-interest
- FINRA OTC corporate actions: https://otce.finra.org/otce/dailyList
- OTC Markets issuer/status pages: https://www.otcmarkets.com/ (use as market/issuer disclosure, not regulator endorsement)
- SEC suspensions and litigation: https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/trading-suspensions and https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/litigation-releases
- Company home regulator and exchange; secretary of state/corporate registry; court dockets; patent office; government procurement and permitting databases

## Sector sources

- ClinicalTrials.gov and API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/ and https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/about-api
- FDA Drugs@FDA and device databases: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/ and https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/medical-device-databases
- Canada SEDAR+ and SEDI: https://www.sedarplus.ca/ and https://www.sedi.ca/
- Mining technical reports: SEC EDGAR, SEDAR+, national/state geological surveys, and permitting agencies

## Secondary and social sources

Use reputable news for context and independent confirmation, then follow links to originals. Use company investor relations for claims and document discovery. Use r/pennystocks, Stocktwits, X, Discord, YouTube, newsletters, and screeners only for discovery, sentiment, promotion mapping, or data that is independently verified.

## Source warnings

- Data vendors often lag share counts, corporate actions, filings, floats, and short interest.
- Analyst targets may be stale, sponsored, based on post-split history, or unsupported by liquidity.
- Press-release distribution through a major news portal does not make the issuer claim independently verified.
- Search snippets and generative summaries are leads, not sources.
