# Sector checklists

Apply the general protocol first, then the relevant sector checks.

## Biotech and medical devices

- Verify trial ID, sponsor, phase, status, enrollment, primary endpoint, comparator, statistical plan, completion date, posted results, and protocol changes on ClinicalTrials.gov or the relevant registry.
- Verify FDA submissions, acceptance, CRL, approval, designation, panel, recall, or warning directly with FDA records; do not trust third-party “PDUFA calendars” alone.
- Separate preclinical, Phase 1 safety, surrogate response, pivotal efficacy, regulatory acceptance, and approval.
- Model cash through the readout and at least one financing contingency. Check milestone/royalty obligations and patent life.

## Mining and exploration

- Verify mineral claims, ownership/royalties, permits, technical reports, qualified person, resource category, grade, recovery, metallurgy, capex, infrastructure, and financing.
- Use SEDAR+/NI 43-101, SEC S-K 1300, geological surveys, and permitting agencies. Treat drill intercepts without width/orientation/context cautiously.
- Distinguish exploration target, inferred/indicated/measured resource, reserve, PEA, pre-feasibility, feasibility, construction, and production.

## Energy and industrial projects

- Verify acreage/assets, ownership, permits, interconnection/offtake, counterparties, production/capacity, unit economics, capex, and funding.
- Distinguish memorandum, framework, purchase order, backlog, binding contract, notice to proceed, installed capacity, and recognized revenue.
- Check commodity-price sensitivity, hedges, abandonment/environmental liabilities, and customer concentration.

## Technology, AI, crypto, and pivots

- Verify product availability, paying customers, revenue recognition, gross margin, retention, compute/capital needs, patents, and named partnerships from both sides.
- Treat “AI,” “blockchain,” “quantum,” or treasury pivots as narratives until filings show operating proof.
- For crypto exposure, separate token/coin holdings, mining economics, custody, debt, equity issuance, and mark-to-market accounting.

## Distressed, bankruptcy, and delisting

- Identify security priority, bankruptcy case, debtor-in-possession terms, asset sale, plan treatment, liquidation value, exchange deadline, hearings, and cancellation language.
- Do not infer common-equity recovery from a rising quote, short squeeze, or asset-sale headline. Model claims senior to common first.
